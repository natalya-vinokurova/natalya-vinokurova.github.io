Natalya Vinokurova is an Associate Professor of Management at the College of Business at Lehigh University. Her research focuses on understanding problems of innovation diffusion — how new products, technologies, and organizational practices are recognized, categorized, and accepted (or fail to be) by the markets and institutions around them.

Her work has been published in top academic journals, including the *Strategic Management Journal*, *Business History Review*, *Business History*, and *Enterprise & Society*, and has won numerous awards, including the Philip B. Scranton Prize for Best Article in *Enterprise & Society*, the Most Novel Research Award from the Behavioral Strategy Division of the Strategic Management Society, and first prize from the Industry Studies Association for her dissertation.

Natalya is also the recipient of multiple teaching awards and was selected as one of the Top 40 Business School Professors Under 40 by Poets&Quants. She currently serves as an Associate Editor of *Industrial and Corporate Change*, on the editorial boards of *Business History*, *Organization Science*, and *Strategy Science*, and as Chair of the Program Committee for the 2027 Business History Conference.

Before academia, she worked as a management consultant and as an analyst at Capital One.
